# 01 Product Requirements

## Purpose
School Hub is a family-owned, cross-school AI information hub. It brings actual school updates into one parent-facing experience rather than merely linking to separate apps.

## Problem
Parents juggle many school apps, emails and calendars across children and schools. Information is fragmented, access can be uneven between caregivers, and one parent often becomes the default coordinator.

## V1 promise
One mobile-first dashboard showing a unified inbox, child-specific updates, upcoming events, actionable deadlines, and source status. Parents can paste announcements and share screenshots; email forwarding and school calendar feeds follow once the basic workflow works. Every update must show its source and when it was received.

## Primary users
Parents and caregivers with children at multiple schools. Separate authorized parent accounts should eventually access one family workspace, without sharing school passwords.

## P0 user stories
See one feed across schools; filter by child; view events and tasks; paste an announcement; review AI-extracted information before publishing; correct or delete items; distinguish connected, stale and unconnected sources.

## MVP screens
Home (briefing and priorities); Inbox (updates and source links); Calendar (events and deadlines); Share (paste/upload and review); Settings (children, schools, sources and privacy).

## Not in V1
Universal school login, automatic portal scraping, grades/attendance, payments, autonomous form submission, native mobile apps.

## Roadmap
V2: shared tasks, delegation and reminders. V3: proactive preparation suggestions such as spirit-week outfit ideas. V4: permission-based completion of supported school tasks and transactions.

## Prototype acceptance
Mobile-friendly fictional two-child/two-school demo; functioning navigation, child filters and task completion; source labels; obvious DEMO indication; no real integrations represented as live.

## Pilot success criteria
Audit capture of critical updates from accessible sources, duplicate/error rates, weekly usage, time saved, and demonstrated willingness to pay. Never claim complete coverage of disconnected apps.
